Executive summary

The managerial solution in brief.

Management workflow mapping · role design · product prototyping · iterative implementation

01

Problem

The spreadsheet could calculate performance, but it did not support the management work around it. Monitoring, follow-up and employee visibility still depended on manual effort.

02

Approach

Build the solution around two complementary roles: a manager workspace for monitoring the team and guiding improvement, and a private employee workspace for self-awareness and follow-up.

03

Managerial outcome

Daily evidence, sprint evaluation, team monitoring and individual history now work as one continuous performance-management loop.

Key product decisions

How the product supports better performance management.

01

Keep the model, change the work around it

Managerial challenge

The team already understood the KPI model and had two years of useful history. Replacing it would remove trust and context.

Product response

Keep the existing KPIs and history, then simplify how managers move through input, review and reporting.

02

Capture evidence while it is fresh

Managerial challenge

When daily signals are left until sprint close, the manager has to rebuild what happened from memory and scattered notes.

Product response

Give attendance and delivery a focused daily flow, with context stored beside each input.

03

Make sprint close a review, not an admin task

Managerial challenge

Daily tracking and sprint-level evaluation happened in the same working surface, even though they require different decisions.

Product response

Separate the two moments. Daily evidence is ready before the manager reviews quality, collaboration and overall delivery.

04

Give each role the view it needs

Managerial challenge

Managers need to monitor the team, but employees should not have access to one another's performance information.

Product response

Create a manager workspace for the team and a private employee workspace for personal reports and history.

05

Turn performance into a conversation

Managerial challenge

A total score alone does not explain where performance changed or what should be discussed next.

Product response

Keep the KPI breakdown, previous-sprint context and history together so the report can support a useful one-to-one.

The role-based product

One performance process. Two clear points of view.

The manager gets a workspace for action and follow-up. Each employee gets a private report they can understand and use.

Manager view

See the process, not just a final score.
